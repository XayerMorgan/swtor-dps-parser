SWTOR Holocron DPS — Quick Start & Setup Guide

Follow the steps below to turn on combat logging in Star Wars: The Old Republic and see your live DPS, HPS, APM, and raid performance in real time.

1 Turn On Combat Logging in SWTOR

  1. Launch Star Wars: The Old Republic and log into your character.
  2. Press ESC (or Ctrl + P) and click Preferences.
  3. Make sure the GAME tab is selected at the bottom-left of the Preferences window.
  4. Click COMBAT LOGGING in the left-hand category list.
  5. Under General, check the box labeled ☑ Enable combat logging to file.
  6. Click Apply and then OK.
In-game Preferences → Game → Combat Logging: Check "Enable combat logging to file" on the right and click Apply.

2 Where SWTOR Saves Your Combat Log Files (And How to Find Yours)

Important Note on Install Types: Whether you installed SWTOR via Steam (C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Star Wars - The Old Republic), the EA App, or the Standalone Broadsword/BioWare Launcher, SWTOR never writes combat logs into the game's installation folder. Instead, SWTOR asks Windows for your personal My Documents folder and writes timestamped text files (combat_YYYY-MM-DD_HH_MM_SS_######.txt) there.

Depending on how your Windows PC is configured, your logs will be in one of these locations:

A. Standard Local Windows Account:

C:\Users\<YourUsername>\Documents\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs

B. Windows OneDrive Backup (Very Common on Windows 10/11):

C:\Users\<YourUsername>\OneDrive\Documents\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs

C. Relocated Documents Library on a Secondary Drive (D:\, E:\):

D:\Documents\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs   (or D:\Users\<YourUsername>\Documents\...)

💡 Fastest Way to Find Your Exact Folder on Any PC

1. Inside the overlay, click 📂 Folder (or open the ❓ Setup & Help tab) and click 🔍 Auto-Scan My PC. The app checks your Windows Registry Personal shell folder, OneDrive paths, and secondary drives, showing how many combat_*.txt files exist in each location so you can select it in one click.

2. Or press Win + R on your keyboard, paste the command below, and press Enter to open your active Windows Documents folder directly:

powershell -c "ii ([Environment]::GetFolderPath('MyDocuments') + '\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs')"

3 Using the Live In-Game Overlay & Session Analyzer

  • Play in Fullscreen (Windowed) Mode: In SWTOR Preferences → Graphics, set Display Mode to Fullscreen (Windowed). This allows the parser overlay to sit directly on top of your game screen without minimizing SWTOR.
  • Full Session Aggregation (📊 Session) vs. Single Fight (⚔️ Fight): By default, 📊 Session aggregates every fight, skill, and ability across your entire multi-hour play session (tracking both active combat time and total session span). Switch to ⚔️ Fight or click any spike on the Session DPS Curve to inspect an individual pull.
