2. How to Find Your CombatLogs Folder (By Install Type)

  • Important: Regardless of whether you installed SWTOR via Steam (C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Star Wars - The Old Republic), the EA App, or the Standalone Broadsword/BioWare Launcher, SWTOR never writes combat logs into the game's install directory.
  • Instead, SWTOR queries Windows for your personal My Documents folder and writes inside:
    %USERPROFILE%\Documents\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs
  • Windows OneDrive Users: If OneDrive backs up your Documents, Windows automatically redirects SWTOR logs to:
    %USERPROFILE%\OneDrive\Documents\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs
  • Moved Documents Drive (D:\, E:\): If you relocated your Windows Documents library to another drive, look inside D:\Documents\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs.
  • Fastest Trick to Find It: Press Win + R on your keyboard, paste:
    powershell -c "ii ([Environment]::GetFolderPath('MyDocuments') + '\Star Wars - The Old Republic\CombatLogs')"
    and press Enter—Windows will open your exact folder immediately!

3. How to Turn On Combat Logging in SWTOR

  1. While in-game, press ESC (or Ctrl + P) and open Preferences.
  2. Under the Game tab at the bottom, click Combat Logging on the left menu.
  3. Check ☑ Enable combat logging to file on the right side and click Apply.
  4. In Preferences → Graphics, set Display Mode to Fullscreen (Windowed) so this overlay stays pinned on top of your game!
